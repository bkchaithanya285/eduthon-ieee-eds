About EDUTHON

EDUTHON is a 24-hour techno-management hackathon that challenges participants to think, build, and innovate by combining technology, strategy, and management thinking to address real-world problems with a focus on sustainability. The event encourages teamwork, creativity, and practical problem-solving, providing participants with an opportunity to develop impactful solutions within a limited time frame while enhancing their technical and managerial skills.

About Our Community

KARE IEEE Education Society is a student-driven professional community at Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education dedicated to promoting excellence in engineering education. The society fosters innovation, technical competence, leadership, and collaborative learning through IEEE-aligned activities.
